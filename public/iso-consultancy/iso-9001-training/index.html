Overview

What the training covers

ISO 9001 only works if the people who use it understand it. Our courses range from a one-day foundation for everyone, to internal auditor training for the people who check the system, to transition training for companies moving to the new edition.

  • ISO 9001 foundation (awareness) course
  • ISO 9001 internal auditor course
  • Transition to the 2026 edition
  • Workshops for process owners and managers
  • In-house courses at your premises
  • Attendance certificates and course materials

Course path

Courses in the ISO 9001 training path

Choose the level that matches each person's role. We can combine levels in one programme for your team.

Foundation (awareness)

For anyone who needs to understand ISO 9001: what it requires, the vocabulary, and how their own role fits in. Typically one day.

Internal auditor

For staff who will audit your quality management system. Covers planning an audit, collecting evidence, writing findings and following them up, with practical exercises. Typically two days.

Transition to the 2026 edition

For managers and auditors working with ISO 9001:2015 who need to move to the new edition: what changes, what to update, and how to plan the move. Typically one day.

Lead auditor

For people who will lead audit teams. Available on request. Tell us the accreditation or recognition you need and we will confirm what we can deliver.

Course content

What each course covers

Each course combines explanation with exercises, so people practise on realistic examples.

CourseMain topicsPractical work
FoundationThe purpose of ISO 9001, the quality management principles, the process approach, risk-based thinking, the structure of the standard, and documented informationMapping a process from your own business, and matching clauses to everyday work
Internal auditorAudit principles, planning an audit programme, checklists, interviewing, collecting evidence, writing nonconformities, reporting, and following up corrective actionRole-played interviews, a mock audit, and writing findings from case studies
TransitionWhat has changed between editions, how the changes affect your system, a gap analysis approach, updating documents, and agreeing timing with your certification bodyA gap analysis applied to a sample of your own system

Course content is adapted to your sector and to the documents you already use.

Learning outcomes

What participants will be able to do

By the end of the training, participants should be able to:

  • Explain what ISO 9001 requires and why
  • Find the clauses that apply to their own work
  • Plan and carry out an internal audit
  • Collect evidence and write clear, factual findings
  • Raise and follow up corrective actions
  • Prepare for a certification or surveillance audit

Before you attend

Prerequisites

Most people can start at the foundation level. For the other courses we recommend:

  • Foundation: no prior knowledge of ISO 9001 is needed
  • Internal auditor: knowledge of ISO 9001, from the foundation course or similar experience
  • Transition: working knowledge of ISO 9001:2015 and access to your own quality manual or procedures
  • Please bring or share a copy of your procedures if you want the exercises built around them

FAQ

Frequently asked questions

Which ISO 9001 course should I choose?

Choose by role. Everyone who needs to understand the standard takes the foundation course. The people who will audit the system take the internal auditor course. If you hold an ISO 9001:2015 certificate, the transition course covers the move to the new edition.

Can you run the course at our premises?

Yes. In-house courses are the most common. We bring the materials and run the sessions at your site in Riyadh or Jeddah, using your own procedures for the exercises.

Do participants receive a certificate?

Yes, a certificate of attendance. If you need a course with a specific accreditation, tell us when you enquire and we will confirm what we can offer.
