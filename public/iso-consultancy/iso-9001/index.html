What's included

ISO 9001: what we do

ISO 9001 is the international standard for quality management systems. It is widely requested in tenders and supplier approvals.

  • Gap analysis against ISO 9001 (2015 or 2026 edition)
  • Quality manual, procedures and records
  • Process mapping and risk assessment
  • Internal auditor training
  • Management review support
  • Certification audit preparation
We are ISO consultants. Certificates are issued by an accredited certification body of your choice; we prepare you for its audit.

ISO 9001:2026 has been published

The new edition was published in September 2026, replacing ISO 9001:2015. A transition period of about three years is expected, during which 2015 certificates remain valid. New to ISO 9001? We agree the edition with your certification body before work starts. Already certified? We can run a gap analysis against the new edition.

Inside the standard

What ISO 9001 requires, clause by clause

The requirements sit in clauses 4 to 10. This is what each asks for and what we produce with you.

ClauseWhat the standard asks forWhat we do with you
4. Context of the organisationUnderstand your business and interested parties; define the scope and processesScope statement, process map, interested-party register
5. LeadershipTop management commitment, a quality policy, clear rolesQuality policy, responsibilities matrix, management briefing
6. PlanningRisks and opportunities, quality objectives, planning of changesRisk register, measurable objectives with owners and dates
7. SupportPeople, infrastructure, competence, awareness and documented informationTraining matrix, document control, calibration and maintenance records
8. OperationSales, design where applicable, purchasing, delivery, and control of nonconforming outputProcedures and forms for your core processes, supplier evaluation
9. Performance evaluationMonitoring, customer satisfaction, internal audit and management reviewKPIs, internal audit programme and report, management review minutes
10. ImprovementNonconformity, corrective action and continual improvementCorrective action log and a root-cause method your team can use

Clause numbers follow the harmonised structure shared by ISO management system standards, so the same framework carries over to ISO 14001, 45001 and 22000.

The audit

What happens at the certification audit

The certification body, not the consultant, carries out these audits.

AuditPurposeWhen
Stage 1Reviews your documentation and checks you are ready for Stage 2Once the system is documented and running
Stage 2Checks the system is implemented and effective, by interviewing staff and sampling recordsUsually a few weeks after Stage 1
SurveillanceConfirms the system is being maintainedNormally once a year
RecertificationA full review to renew the certificateEvery three years

Avoid these

Common reasons companies get nonconformities

We see the same gaps repeatedly. The internal audit we run before certification is designed to catch them.

  • No internal audit was completed before the certification audit
  • Management review was not held, or not recorded
  • Quality objectives are not measurable
  • Documents in use are out of date or uncontrolled
  • Suppliers are not evaluated
  • Calibration records are missing for measuring equipment
  • Corrective actions are closed without a root cause
  • Staff cannot explain the quality policy or their role in it

FAQ

Frequently asked questions

Is ISO 9001 only for large companies?

No. It applies to companies of any size and sector.

What documents will I need?

At least a scope, policy, objectives, procedures and records that match your processes. We build them with you.

Should we certify to the 2015 or the 2026 edition?

It depends on your timing and your certification body. We check the current transition rules with them at the start so you do not have to redo work.

We are already certified to ISO 9001:2015. What should we do?

Your certificate remains valid during the transition period. Plan a gap analysis against the 2026 edition and update the system ahead of a scheduled audit.

How many staff need to be involved?

A management representative and the owners of your main processes. We keep their time to short working sessions.

Does ISO 9001 require a quality manual?

A manual is not mandatory, but you do need documented information that describes your system. Many companies keep a short manual because customers ask for it.

Can the audit be done remotely?

Certification bodies can audit some activities remotely, depending on their rules and your operations. They decide, not the consultant.
