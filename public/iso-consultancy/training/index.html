ISO Training: what we do

A management system only works if your own people can run it. We train staff to understand the standard, audit the system and keep it going after certification.

  • ISO awareness for all staff
  • Internal auditor courses
  • HACCP and food safety training
  • HSE and workplace safety
  • Integrated Pest Management for food and hospitality teams
  • Training records for your audit file
We are ISO consultants. Certificates are issued by an accredited certification body of your choice; we prepare you for its audit.

Why train

Short and focused

Records for the auditor

A trained internal team

FAQ

Frequently asked questions

Where is the training held?

At your premises or at a venue we arrange, in Riyadh or Jeddah. Group size and dates are agreed when you book.

Do participants receive a certificate?

Yes, a certificate of attendance. If you need a course with a specific accreditation, tell us and we will confirm the options.

Can training be combined with an ISO project?

Yes. Most projects include awareness and internal auditor training, so your team can run the system afterwards.

How many people can attend?

Group size depends on the course. We plan it with you to keep exercises practical.

Do you train in Arabic?

Tell us the language your team needs when you enquire, and we will confirm what we can deliver.
