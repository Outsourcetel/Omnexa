Which ISO standard do you need?

The right standard depends on your industry and on what your customers and tenders ask for.

StandardWhat it coversWho usually needs it
ISO 9001 QualityConsistent delivery and customer satisfactionAlmost any company. Commonly requested in tenders and supplier approvals
ISO 22000 Food safetyControl of food-safety hazards across the food chain, including HACCP principlesFood manufacturers, caterers, central kitchens, packers and cold stores
ISO 45001 Health and safetyWorkplace hazards, incident prevention and legal complianceContractors, factories, and facility and maintenance companies
ISO 14001 EnvironmentWaste, emissions, resource use and compliance obligationsManufacturers, contractors, and suppliers to clients with environmental requirements
ISO 27001 Information securityRisk assessment and controls to protect informationIT, software, finance and outsourcing firms, and any company that handles client data

Many companies combine two or three standards in one integrated management system, which reduces duplicated documents and audit days.

ISO 9001 and ISO 14001 have new 2026 editions

ISO 14001:2026 was published in April 2026 and ISO 9001:2026 in September 2026. Certificates to the 2015 editions remain valid during a transition of about three years. If you are starting now, we plan your project against the new editions; if you are already certified, we can run a gap analysis and prepare your transition.

Who does what

Consultant, certification body, accreditation body

We are the consultant

We run the gap analysis, build the system with your team, train your staff, carry out the internal audit and prepare you for the certification audit.

The certification body audits you

An independent body audits in two stages: Stage 1 checks your documentation and readiness, Stage 2 checks that the system is working. It issues the certificate.

The accreditation body checks the certifier

A credible certificate comes from a certification body accredited by a recognised accreditation body, such as the Saudi Accreditation Center or the GCC Accreditation Center.

After certification

The certificate normally runs for three years, with surveillance audits each year and a recertification audit at the end. We can support those too.

The certification body's process

What the certification body does at its end

These steps are the certification body's, not ours. Knowing them in advance avoids surprises.

StepWhat happensWho acts
1. ApplicationYou complete the certification body's application form with company details, scope and the standardYou, with our help
2. Proposal, contract and feesIt sends a proposal and contract, and you pay the agreed certification feesCertification body and you
3. Audit programmeIt plans the dates and assigns an audit teamCertification body
4. Stage 1 and Stage 2 auditsStage 1 reviews your documents and readiness; Stage 2 checks the system works in practiceCertification body
5. DecisionThe audit reports and your corrective actions go to a decision-maker who was not on the audit team. If approved, the certificate is issuedCertification body

Cost

What ISO certification costs

There are two separate costs, plus the ongoing ones. We give a fixed written quotation for our part after the free consultation.

  • Consultancy fee: depends on the standard, number of sites, headcount, and how much you already have in place
  • Certification body fee: based on audit days, which are set by headcount, sites and the risk level of your activity
  • Annual surveillance audits by the certification body
  • A recertification audit every three years
  • Your own staff time to run the system

Buyer's guide

How to avoid a worthless certificate

Certificates from non-accredited bodies are often rejected in tenders. Before you pay anyone, check:

  • The certification body is accredited, and the accreditation covers the standard and your sector
  • The certificate can be verified on the certification body's public register
  • A real audit takes place, on site or remote, with an audit report
  • Nobody is promising a certificate within days or a guaranteed pass
  • The consultant and the certification body are separate organisations

Your information stays confidential

To assess your system we need to see your processes, customers and sometimes your costs. We treat everything you share as confidential, and are happy to sign a confidentiality agreement before the gap analysis.

Our certification partner

We work with ACERTA Certification & Inspection as a certification partner. You are free to choose any accredited certification body, and we prepare you for its audit whichever you choose. We do not take part in the audit or influence the certification decision.

FAQ

Frequently asked questions

Are you a certification body?

No. We are consultants. We help you implement the standard and prepare. The certificate is issued by an accredited certification body that you choose.

How long does ISO certification take?

It varies with company size, existing processes and the standard. We give a timeline after the gap analysis.

Which ISO standard do I need?

It depends on your industry and customers. ISO 9001 suits most companies; food businesses often need ISO 22000; contractors often need ISO 45001 and 14001.

Do you work outside Riyadh and Jeddah?

Yes. We work with companies across Saudi Arabia and the Gulf. Some stages can be done remotely.

Can you guarantee we will be certified?

No honest consultant can, because the decision belongs to the independent certification body. What we do is prepare you thoroughly and stay with you through the audit and any findings.

Can small companies get certified?

Yes. The standards apply to organisations of any size, and a small company's system can be short and simple.

Can we combine several standards?

Yes. ISO 9001, 14001 and 45001 share the same structure, so they are often implemented together as one integrated system.

Do you provide training?

Yes. Awareness training for staff and internal auditor training are part of most projects.
