ISO Consultants in Saudi Arabia: what we do

Omnexa Solutions provides ISO consultancy: gap analysis, documentation, implementation, internal audit support and certification preparation. Certificates are issued by accredited certification bodies, and we prepare you for their audits.

  • ISO 9001 Quality Management
  • ISO 22000 Food Safety Management
  • ISO 45001 Occupational Health and Safety
  • ISO 14001 Environmental Management
  • ISO 27001 Information Security
  • Staff training and internal auditor training
  • Surveillance audit support
  • Support for tenders that require ISO
We are ISO consultants. Certificates are issued by an accredited certification body of your choice; we prepare you for its audit.

How it works

  1. Gap analysisWe review your current processes against the standard and list what is missing.

Frequently asked questions

Are you a certification body?

No. We are consultants. We help you implement the standard and prepare. The certificate is issued by an accredited certification body that you choose.

How long does ISO certification take?

It varies with company size, existing processes and the standard. We give a timeline after the gap analysis.

Which ISO standard do I need?

It depends on your industry and customers. ISO 9001 suits most companies; food businesses often need ISO 22000; contractors often need ISO 45001 and 14001.

Do you work outside Riyadh?

Yes. We work with companies across Saudi Arabia and the Gulf. Some stages can be done remotely.
