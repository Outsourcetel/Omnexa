How we work

What working with us in Jeddah looks like

We start with a free consultation to understand what your customers or tenders are asking for and which standard answers it.

The gap analysis, workshops, staff training and internal audit are done at your premises, with your team. Document drafting and reviews happen between visits, so the time we ask of your managers stays short.

When the system is ready, you appoint an accredited certification body and we stay with you through its audit and any findings.

Process

How it works

  1. Gap analysisWe review your current processes against the standard and list what is missing.
  2. Documentation and implementationWe help you write the procedures and put them into practice.
  3. Internal audit and reviewWe support an internal audit and management review before the external audit.
  4. Certification audit supportYou select an accredited certification body, and we prepare you for its audit.
