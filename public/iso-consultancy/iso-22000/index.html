ISO 22000: what we do

ISO 22000 sets requirements for a food safety management system across the food chain. It is relevant to manufacturers, caterers, packers and storage operators.

  • Documentation and records

HACCP, ISO 22000 or FSSC 22000?

The three are related. Which one you need depends on who is asking.

SchemeWhat it isWho usually asks for it
HACCPA method for identifying food-safety hazards and controlling them at critical pointsThe baseline for any food business
ISO 22000A full food-safety management system that includes HACCP principles, plus management, communication and improvement requirementsCustomers and tenders that want a certified management system
FSSC 22000A certification scheme built on ISO 22000 with additional sector requirements, recognised by the Global Food Safety InitiativeLarge retailers, multinational brands and export customers

We can implement ISO 22000 so that moving up to FSSC 22000 later does not mean starting again.

Inside the standard

What ISO 22000 requires

The current edition is ISO 22000:2018. The main elements are:

  • Prerequisite programmes: cleaning, pest control, personal hygiene, maintenance, supplier approval
  • Hazard analysis for each product and process
  • Critical control points and operational prerequisite programmes, with limits and monitoring
  • Traceability, and a tested withdrawal and recall procedure
  • Emergency preparedness
  • Verification, internal audit and management review

Who it is for

A system your team runs daily, with the evidence an auditor asks for.

  • Food-safety policy and objectives
  • Prerequisite programme procedures
  • Process flow diagrams
  • Hazard analysis and the hazard control plan
  • Monitoring records for critical limits
  • Traceability and recall procedure, with a mock recall record
  • Supplier approval records
  • Training records
  • Internal audit report and management review minutes

Prerequisite programmes come first

Cleaning, pest control, maintenance, personal hygiene and supplier approval must be working before the hazard analysis is credible. We review each one in the gap analysis and tell you which records an auditor will expect to see.

FAQ

Frequently asked questions

What is the link between ISO 22000 and HACCP?

ISO 22000 includes HACCP principles within a wider management system.

Do we need HACCP before ISO 22000?

No. HACCP is built as part of the ISO 22000 project. If you already have it, we use what you have.

Is ISO 22000 suitable for a restaurant?

It can be, especially for groups and central kitchens. A single small restaurant often starts with HACCP.

Does ISO 22000 replace food authority requirements?

No. It works alongside the legal and municipal requirements that apply to your business, and helps you show you meet them.

How long does it take?

Often four to eight months for a manufacturer, depending on the number of products and how much is already in place.
