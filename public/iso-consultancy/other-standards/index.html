Other ISO Standards: what we do

Beyond ISO 9001, 14001, 45001, 22000 and 27001, companies are often asked for other standards or customer schemes. The method is the same: we find the gaps, build the system with your team, audit it internally, and prepare you for the certification body.

  • ISO 50001 energy management
  • ISO 22301 business continuity
  • ISO 20000-1 IT service management
  • ISO 37001 anti-bribery
  • ISO 13485 medical devices
  • HACCP, FSSC 22000, BRCGS, GMP and Halal
  • Supplier ethics and sustainability audits
  • Export product compliance
We are ISO consultants. Certificates are issued by an accredited certification body of your choice; we prepare you for its audit.

FAQ

Frequently asked questions

Can you help with a standard that is not listed?

Probably. Tell us the standard or scheme and who is asking for it. If it needs a specialist we will say so, and tell you who can help.

Do you carry out the certification?

No. We are consultants. Certification is by an accredited certification body, or by the scheme owner for customer schemes.

Can we combine several standards?

Yes, where they share a structure, which many ISO management system standards do. One integrated system saves documents and audit days.

How long does ISO 50001 or ISO 22301 take?

Often three to six months, depending on size and how much you already measure or plan. We give a dated plan after the gap analysis.

Do we need ISO 9001 first?

Not required. But if you have ISO 9001, the new system can reuse its processes, documents and audits.
