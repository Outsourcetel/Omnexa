How ISO certification works with us

From the first meeting to the certificate and beyond: what happens, who does what, and who decides at each step.

WhatsApp usCall +966 55 818 7808
Steps1. Free consultation2. Gap analysis3. Design and documentation4. Implementation and training5. Internal audit and review6. Certification audit7. Maintain and renew
1
One meeting

Free consultation

What we do

Understand why you need ISO (a tender, a customer, growth), agree the right standard and the scope.

What you do

Share the tender or customer requirement, your headcount and your sites.

What you get

A recommended standard, a rough timeline and a written quotation.

2
Typically 1 to 2 weeks

Gap analysis

What we do

Compare your current practice with the standard, interview managers, and list every gap.

What you do

Give access to managers and to the documents you already use.

What you get

A gap report and a dated project plan.

3
Typically 4 to 8 weeks

Design and documentation

What we do

Write the policy, process maps, procedures and forms with your team, in your own words and workflow.

What you do

Name process owners, review drafts and approve them.

What you get

Your management system documents.

4
Typically 4 to 8 weeks

Implementation and training

What we do

Train staff and internal auditors, and support your team while the system starts running.

What you do

Run the processes and keep the records.

What you get

A trained team and a running system with records.

5
Typically 2 to 3 weeks

Internal audit and review

What we do

Audit the system against the standard, report findings, and support corrective actions.

What you do

Fix the findings and hold the management review.

What you get

An internal audit report and management review minutes.

6
Scheduled by the certification body

Certification audit

What we do

Help you choose an accredited certification body, prepare you, and support you through the audit and any findings.

What you do

Host the auditors. The certification body, not us, decides.

What you get

A certificate from the certification body, if you meet the standard.

7
Three-year certificate cycle

Maintain and renew

What we do

Support annual surveillance audits and prepare you for recertification.

What you do

Keep operating the system and keep the records up to date.

What you get

A certificate that stays valid.
