ISO 27001: what we do

ISO 27001 sets requirements for managing information security risks. It is requested by many IT, finance and outsourcing buyers.

  • Scope and risk assessment
  • Statement of Applicability
  • Policies and controls
  • Awareness training
  • Internal audit
  • Certification audit preparation
We are ISO consultants. Certificates are issued by an accredited certification body of your choice; we prepare you for its audit.

Inside the standard

How ISO 27001 is structured

The current edition is ISO 27001:2022. It has management system clauses plus a catalogue of 93 security controls in four groups.

PartWhat it coversWhat we do with you
Clauses 4 to 10Scope, leadership, risk assessment, objectives, operation, audit and improvementScope, risk methodology, risk register and treatment plan
Organisational controls (37)Policies, asset management, access control, supplier security, incident managementPolicies and procedures that match how you work
People controls (8)Screening, awareness training, responsibilities, remote workingAwareness programme and onboarding and offboarding steps
Physical controls (14)Secure areas, equipment protection, clear deskSite review and practical physical measures
Technological controls (34)Authentication, backup, logging, malware protection, secure development, encryptionA gap list for your IT team and evidence for the auditor

You choose which controls apply to you and justify the choice in a Statement of Applicability, which the auditor checks.

Local context

ISO 27001 and Saudi requirements

ISO 27001 does not replace Saudi requirements such as the National Cybersecurity Authority's controls or the Personal Data Protection Law. What it gives you is the structure, the risk assessment and the evidence that make those requirements much easier to meet and to demonstrate.

If you are subject to specific regulations, tell us at the start and we will map them into the system so one set of controls serves both.

Avoid these

Common gaps we find

These come up at most first audits.

  • The scope is too vague, or leaves out systems that hold client data
  • The risk assessment was done once and never updated
  • Access rights are not reviewed when staff change role or leave
  • Backups exist but restoring from them has never been tested
  • Suppliers with access to data have no security terms
  • Staff have had no security awareness training

FAQ

Frequently asked questions

Is ISO 27001 only for IT companies?

No. Any organisation handling sensitive information can use it.

Do we need to apply all 93 controls?

No. You apply the controls your risk assessment calls for and justify any you exclude in the Statement of Applicability.

Is this an IT project?

IT is central, but the system also covers people, suppliers and physical security. Management has to own it.

How long does it take?

Commonly four to eight months, depending on your scope and how mature your IT practices are.

Do you carry out penetration testing?

No. We are management system consultants. We tell you where technical testing is needed and work with your IT team or their provider.
