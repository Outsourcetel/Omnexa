ISO 45001: what we do

ISO 45001 helps organisations manage health and safety risks. It is commonly requested of contractors and industrial companies.

  • Hazard identification and risk assessment
  • Policies, procedures and records
  • Emergency preparedness
  • HSE training
  • Internal audit
  • Certification audit preparation
We are ISO consultants. Certificates are issued by an accredited certification body of your choice; we prepare you for its audit.

Inside the standard

Background

ISO 45001 replaced OHSAS 18001

ISO 45001 took over from OHSAS 18001, which has been withdrawn. If a tender or client document still refers to OHSAS 18001, ISO 45001 is the certificate they now expect.

It uses the same structure as ISO 9001 and ISO 14001, so the three are often implemented together as one integrated system with shared procedures, one internal audit programme and one management review.

Avoid these

Common gaps we find

These are the findings that come up most often at certification audits.

  • Risk assessments copied from a template and not specific to the site
  • Workers were never consulted on hazards or controls
  • No emergency drills, or drills with no record
  • Contractors working without induction or control
  • Near misses are not reported, so nothing is learned from them
  • The legal register is out of date

FAQ

Frequently asked questions

Does it replace local regulations?

No. It is a management system standard and works alongside legal requirements.

Is ISO 45001 mandatory?

It is not a legal requirement, but many clients and tenders require it from contractors and suppliers.

Can it be combined with ISO 9001 and 14001?

Yes. The three share a structure, and an integrated system reduces documents and audit time.

Do we need a full-time safety officer?

The standard requires competent people and clear responsibilities, not a specific job title. Your clients or regulations may ask for more.

How long does it take?

Commonly three to six months, depending on the number of sites and the level of risk in your work.
