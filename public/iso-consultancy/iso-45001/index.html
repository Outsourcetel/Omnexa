ISO 45001 Health and Safety Consultants: what we do

ISO 45001 helps organisations manage health and safety risks. It is commonly requested of contractors and industrial companies.

  • Hazard identification and risk assessment
  • Policies, procedures and records
  • Emergency preparedness
  • HSE training
  • Internal audit
  • Certification audit preparation
We are ISO consultants. Certificates are issued by an accredited certification body of your choice; we prepare you for its audit.

How it works

  1. Gap analysisWe review your current processes against the standard and list what is missing.
  2. Documentation and implementationWe help you write the procedures and put them into practice.
  3. Internal audit and reviewWe support an internal audit and management review before the external audit.
  4. Certification audit supportYou select an accredited certification body, and we prepare you for its audit.

Frequently asked questions

Does it replace local regulations?

No. It is a management system standard and works alongside legal requirements.
