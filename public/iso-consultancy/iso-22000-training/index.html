Overview

What the training covers

Food safety depends on every person who handles food. Our courses cover food safety awareness for all staff, HACCP team training for those who run the hazard analysis, and internal auditor training for ISO 22000.

  • Food safety foundation (awareness) course
  • HACCP team training
  • ISO 22000 internal auditor course
  • Prerequisite programmes and traceability sessions
  • In-house courses at your premises
  • Attendance certificates and course materials

Course path

Courses in the ISO 22000 training path

Choose the level that matches each person's role. We can combine levels in one programme for your team.

Foundation (awareness)

For food handlers, supervisors and managers: hygiene, hazards, prerequisite programmes and what ISO 22000 requires. Typically one day.

HACCP team training

For the team that will carry out the hazard analysis: the HACCP principles, flow diagrams, critical control points and monitoring. Typically two days.

Internal auditor

For staff who will audit the food safety system, including prerequisite programmes and HACCP records. Typically two days.

Lead auditor

For people who will lead audit teams. Available on request. Tell us the accreditation or recognition you need and we will confirm what we can deliver.

Learning outcomes

What participants will be able to do

By the end of the training, participants should be able to:

  • Explain what ISO 22000 and HACCP require and why
  • Identify food safety hazards in a process
  • Set and monitor critical control points
  • Plan and carry out an internal audit of the food safety system
  • Check traceability and recall readiness
  • Prepare for a certification or surveillance audit

FAQ

Frequently asked questions

Which food safety course should I choose?

Choose by role. Food handlers and managers take the foundation course. The team that writes the hazard analysis takes HACCP team training. Those who will audit the system take the internal auditor course.

Do we need HACCP training before ISO 22000?

HACCP is built as part of the ISO 22000 system. If your team already knows HACCP, we start from what they know.

Can you run the course at our premises?

Yes. In-house courses are the most common. We bring the materials and run the sessions at your site in Riyadh or Jeddah, using your own procedures for the exercises.

Do participants receive a certificate?

Yes, a certificate of attendance. If you need a course with a specific accreditation, tell us when you enquire and we will confirm what we can offer.
