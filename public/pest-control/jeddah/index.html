Local conditions

Pest pressure in Jeddah

Jeddah is humid for most of the year, and humidity suits the pests people complain about most. Cockroaches breed faster, mosquitoes find standing water, and termites stay active in damp soil.

As a port city, Jeddah also handles large volumes of stored food and goods. Warehouses and containers bring stored-product insects and rodents, which is why monitoring and fumigation are a bigger part of our work here.

FAQ

Frequently asked questions

Which parts of Jeddah do you cover?

We cover the city, its industrial areas and the port area. Tell us your district when you enquire and we will confirm the earliest available visit.

Do you fumigate containers and warehouses?

Yes. Send us the commodity, volume and timing and we will prepare a fumigation plan and quotation.

Does humidity make pests worse?

Yes. Cockroaches, mosquitoes and termites all do well in humid conditions, so follow-up visits and prevention matter more on the coast.
