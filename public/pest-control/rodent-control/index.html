Rodent Control in Riyadh: what we do

Rodents carry contamination risks and damage cables, stock and structures. We find how they get in, treat the activity, and advise on proofing so they do not return.

  • Inspection and entry-point identification
  • Baiting and trapping, placed away from food and children
  • Proofing recommendations
  • Monitoring stations for commercial sites
  • Service reports for audits
