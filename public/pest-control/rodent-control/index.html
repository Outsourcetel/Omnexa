Early warning

Signs of rodents

Rodents are active at night, so you usually see the evidence before the animal.

  • Droppings along walls, in cupboards or behind equipment
  • Gnaw marks on packaging, cables, doors and pipes
  • Greasy rub marks along regular runs
  • Scratching sounds in ceilings or walls at night
  • Nests of shredded paper, cardboard or insulation
  • A strong, stale smell in closed stores

Our approach

Four parts to getting rid of rodents

Inspect and identify

We find the species, the entry points, the runs and what is attracting them.

Proof the building

Gaps are sealed, door sweeps and drain covers fitted, and vents meshed, so new rodents cannot replace the old ones.

Control

Tamper-resistant bait stations and traps are placed along runs, numbered and fixed in position.

Monitor

Stations are checked on a schedule, activity is recorded, and the plan is adjusted until activity stops.

Prevention

What you can do between visits

Rodents stay where they find food, water and shelter. Removing those is half the job.

  • Store food and animal feed in sealed containers
  • Keep waste in lidded bins and remove it daily
  • Clear clutter, pallets and unused cardboard from stores
  • Fix leaking pipes and remove standing water
  • Keep external doors closed and fit door sweeps
  • Cut back plants and trees that touch the building

Food premises need a different plan

In kitchens, production and food storage areas we rely on traps and non-toxic monitoring, and keep rodenticide baits in secured stations outside those areas. Every station is mapped, so an inspector or auditor can check each one.

FAQ

Frequently asked questions

Is rodent control safe around food areas?

We plan placement around food handling and storage areas and use monitoring methods suited to the site.

Do you offer regular contracts?

Yes. Restaurants, warehouses and food facilities usually need scheduled visits and monitoring.

How long does it take to clear rodents?

It depends on the species and how established they are. Mice in a small property can be cleared in a couple of weeks; rats in a large site take longer. We set expectations after the inspection.

Are the bait stations safe around children and pets?

Stations are tamper-resistant, locked and fixed in place. Tell us about children and pets and we will choose positions and methods to suit.

Will you seal the entry holes?

We identify entry points and carry out or advise on proofing, depending on the type of work needed.

What about dead rodents and smell?

We check stations and traps on follow-up visits and remove what we find. Proofing and trapping reduce the chance of rodents dying in hidden voids.
