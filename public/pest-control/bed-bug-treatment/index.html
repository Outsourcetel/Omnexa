Early warning

Signs of bed bugs

Bites alone do not confirm bed bugs. Look for these signs together:

  • Bites in lines or small clusters, often noticed in the morning
  • Small blood spots on sheets and pillowcases
  • Dark spots along mattress seams, the headboard and bed frame
  • Pale shed skins and tiny white eggs in seams and joints
  • Live insects, flat, reddish brown and about the size of an apple seed
  • A sweet, musty smell in heavy infestations

How it happens

How bed bugs spread

Bed bugs travel in luggage, used furniture and bedding, and move between adjoining rooms through gaps, pipes and electrical conduits. They are not a sign of poor hygiene: they turn up in clean homes and good hotels.

They hide within a few metres of where people sleep and feed at night. Adults can survive for months without feeding, so leaving a room empty does not clear them.

Eggs are resistant to most treatments and hatch about one to two weeks later. That is why a follow-up visit is part of every bed bug treatment.

Before we arrive

Preparation checklist

Bed bug treatment depends on preparation more than any other pest. We send this list when you book.

  • Wash bedding and clothes on a hot cycle and dry them on high heat
  • Seal washed items in clean bags until treatment is finished
  • Reduce clutter around and under the bed
  • Do not move items to other rooms: it spreads the problem
  • Vacuum the mattress, bed frame and floor, then seal and dispose of the bag
  • Do not throw away the mattress before we inspect it: it can often be treated

FAQ

Frequently asked questions

Do I need to wash bedding first?

We give a preparation checklist when you book, including bedding and clothing.

Can you treat a whole hotel or camp?

Yes. We plan room-by-room treatment for hotels and staff accommodation.

Can I treat bed bugs myself?

Shop-bought sprays rarely reach the hiding places or the eggs, and can spread the insects to other rooms. Professional treatment with a follow-up is more reliable.

Do I have to throw away my mattress?

Usually not. Most mattresses can be treated. We advise after inspecting.

How soon can we sleep in the room?

We tell you the re-entry time on the day. It depends on the method used.

How do I avoid bringing them home again?

Check hotel beds when you travel, keep luggage off the bed and floor, and wash travel clothes on a hot cycle when you return.
