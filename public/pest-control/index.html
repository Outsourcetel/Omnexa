Pest Control in Riyadh: what we do

Omnexa Solutions provides pest control and Integrated Pest Management (IPM) programmes in Riyadh and across Saudi Arabia. We start with an inspection, recommend a treatment plan, and keep service records for every visit.

  • General insect control (cockroaches, ants, flies)
  • Termite control (pre- and post-construction)
  • Rodent control
  • Bed bug treatment
  • Fumigation for warehouses and stored goods
  • Bird and reptile control
  • Mosquito control
  • Scheduled contracts for commercial sites

How it works

  1. Site inspectionA technician inspects the premises, identifies the pest, entry points and conditions that attract it.
  2. Treatment planYou get a written plan with the method, products, and any preparation needed.
  3. TreatmentTreatment is carried out with minimal disruption to your operations or household.
  4. Follow-up and recordsFollow-up visits and service reports, so you can show what was done.
