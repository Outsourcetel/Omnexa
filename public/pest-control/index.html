Before and after

How to prepare for a treatment

A little preparation makes treatment more effective. We confirm the exact steps for your site when you book.

  • Tell us about children, pets, fish tanks, pregnancy or respiratory conditions in the household
  • Clear the areas to be treated: under sinks, kitchen cupboards, storage rooms
  • Cover or put away food, utensils and children's toys
  • Keep people and pets out of treated rooms for the period we advise
  • Afterwards, ventilate as instructed and avoid mopping treated edges for the period we advise
  • Keep the service report: it lists what was used and where

Check your provider's licence

In Saudi Arabia, a company offering public health pest control needs a qualification issued through the Balady municipal platform. Public health pesticides also carry an SFDA registration number on the label. Ask any provider to show you both before work starts.

FAQ

Frequently asked questions

Do you offer a free inspection?

Yes. Request a free site inspection using the form, WhatsApp or phone, and we will arrange a visit.

Do you serve businesses as well as homes?

Yes. We serve homes, restaurants, hotels, healthcare sites, warehouses and food facilities, including scheduled contracts.

Is the treatment safe for people and pets?

Treatment methods and products depend on the pest and the site. Tell us about children, pets or food areas when you book, and we will plan around them.

Will you give us service reports?

Yes. We provide service records after visits, which are useful for audits and inspections.

How long does a treatment take?

A typical apartment or villa treatment takes one to two hours. Larger sites and termite work take longer. We confirm the time when we quote.

How soon can we use the rooms again?

It depends on the product and method. We tell you the re-entry time for each area before we start, and it is written on the service report.

Do you offer contracts for restaurants and warehouses?

Yes. Contracts include scheduled visits, monitoring points and a service report after every visit.

Will one treatment be enough?

For some pests, yes. Cockroaches, bed bugs and rodents usually need a follow-up visit because eggs hatch or new activity appears. We tell you what to expect in the quotation.
