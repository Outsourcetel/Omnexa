Fumigation: what we do

Fumigation is used for stored-product pests and difficult infestations. It requires planning, sealing, safe handling and clearance before re-entry.

  • Site assessment and fumigation plan
  • Warehouse and storage fumigation
  • Container and commodity fumigation
  • Safety and re-entry clearance procedures
  • Treatment records

How it works

  1. Site inspectionA technician inspects the premises, identifies the pest, entry points and conditions that attract it.
  2. Treatment planYou get a written plan with the method, products, and any preparation needed.
