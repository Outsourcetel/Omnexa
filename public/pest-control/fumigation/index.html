Fumigation: what we do

Fumigation is used for stored-product pests and difficult infestations. It requires planning, sealing, safe handling and clearance before re-entry.

  • Site assessment and fumigation plan
  • Warehouse and storage fumigation
  • Container and commodity fumigation
  • Safety and re-entry clearance procedures
  • Treatment records

Scope

What can be fumigated

Warehouses and stores

Whole buildings or sealed sections that hold infested goods.

Stored commodities

Grain, rice, flour, dates and other products, treated in stacks under gas-proof sheets.

Shipping containers

Loaded containers before dispatch or on arrival.

Wooden packaging and pallets

Timber that carries wood-boring insects.

Is it what you need?

Fumigation is not the same as spraying or fogging

Spraying and fogging treat surfaces and the air in a room. Fumigation uses a gas that penetrates into the commodity itself, into sacks, pallets and voids, and reaches insects at every life stage.

Because it is a gas, fumigation leaves no lasting protection once the space has been aired. It solves an existing infestation; it does not prevent the next one. For that reason we usually pair it with monitoring and hygiene advice.

Fumigants are hazardous and must only be handled by trained, licensed fumigators. If a provider proposes fumigation without sealing the space or testing the air before re-entry, do not proceed.

Fumigation is priced on volume, commodity and time. Send us:

Safety comes first

Nobody enters a fumigated space until the air has been tested and we have issued clearance in writing. Neighbouring units are told in advance, and warning signs stay in place for the whole exposure and aeration period.

FAQ

Frequently asked questions

Is the site closed during fumigation?

Yes. The area is sealed and re-entry follows a clearance procedure. We plan timing with you.

Do you need notice?

Fumigation requires scheduling and preparation. Contact us with the site details for a plan and quotation.

How long does fumigation take?

It commonly takes several days including exposure and aeration. The time depends on the fumigant, the temperature and the commodity. We confirm it in the plan.

Does fumigation leave residues on food?

After proper aeration, the gas disperses. We follow the product label for each commodity and test the air before clearance.

Can staff keep working nearby?

Not in or next to the sealed area. We agree an exclusion zone with you before work starts.

Will the insects come back?

Fumigation gives no lasting protection, so goods can be re-infested. We advise on storage hygiene and monitoring afterwards.
