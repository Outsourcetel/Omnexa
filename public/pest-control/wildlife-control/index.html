Gardens, compounds, farms and warehouses can attract snakes, lizards and stray cats and dogs. These are best handled by trained people, with attention to safety for residents and for the animals.

  • Safe capture and removal of snakes by trained staff

If you see a snake

Safety steps

Most snake bites happen when people try to catch or kill the snake.

  • Keep still, then move slowly away
  • Keep children and pets back
  • Do not try to catch, hit or trap it
  • Note where it went
  • Call us with the location
  • If someone is bitten, seek medical help immediately

Prevention

What attracts them, and how to reduce it

Animals come for food, water and shelter. Removing those reduces visits.

  • Remove rubble, wood piles and dense ground cover next to buildings
  • Control rodents, because snakes follow them
  • Keep waste in lidded bins and clear food waste daily
  • Seal gaps under doors and around pipes
  • Trim vegetation away from walls and fences
  • Fix leaking taps and standing water

FAQ

Frequently asked questions

What should I do if I see a snake?

Keep away, keep children and pets back, and call us. Do not try to catch or kill it: most bites happen when people try.

Why do I have lizards in the kitchen?

They follow the insects that follow food and light. Reducing insects, sealing gaps and fitting door sweeps reduces lizards.

Can you remove stray animals?

We advise and assist in compounds and business sites. Where an authority is responsible for stray animals, we coordinate with them.

Do you handle snakes in farms and large grounds?

We can inspect and advise on large sites. Capture depends on access and safety, so we assess it first.

Is removal humane?

Our aim is safe capture and removal with minimal harm, and advice that stops animals coming back.
