Know your pest

Which cockroach is it?

Two species cause most problems, and they need different treatment.

TypeHow to recognise itWhere it livesWhat works
German cockroachSmall, about 12 to 15 mm, light brown with two dark stripes behind the headIndoors, close to food, warmth and moisture: kitchens, equipment motors, cupboardsGel baits in cracks and hiding places, growth regulators, a follow-up visit
American cockroachLarge, about 35 to 40 mm, reddish brownDrains, sewers, manholes, basements and bathrooms; it enters through drains and gapsDrain and manhole treatment, residual treatment at entry points, sealing gaps

Why sprays fail

Why spraying alone does not solve it

Cockroaches spend most of their time hidden in cracks, inside equipment and in drains, where a surface spray does not reach them.

Their egg cases protect the eggs from most insecticides, so a new generation hatches after the adults have died. That is why a single treatment often looks successful for two weeks and then fails.

Gel baits work differently: cockroaches feed on the bait in their hiding places and carry it back to others. Combined with drain treatment and a follow-up visit timed to the hatching cycle, this breaks the breeding cycle instead of scattering it.

Where cockroaches hide in a kitchen

These are the places we check first, and the places a quick spray misses.

  • Motor housings of fridges, freezers and dishwashers
  • Under and behind cooking equipment
  • Floor drains and grease traps
  • Cardboard boxes and delivery packaging
  • Electrical boxes, switch plates and cable trunking
  • Door seals, hinges and hollow table legs

Before we arrive

How to prepare

Preparation makes the treatment reach where it needs to.

  • Empty the cupboards under sinks and around affected areas
  • Clean grease and food residue from behind equipment where you can
  • Remove unused cardboard boxes
  • Cover or put away food and utensils
  • Do not use household sprays before or after gel baiting: they repel cockroaches from the bait

FAQ

Frequently asked questions

How quickly do I see results?

It depends on the infestation and the site. We advise on expectations after inspecting.

Do I need to leave the property?

Sometimes for a short time. We tell you what preparation is needed before treatment.

Why am I seeing more cockroaches after the treatment?

Treatment disturbs them and draws them out of hiding. Activity for a few days afterwards is normal and should then fall.

Is gel bait safe in a kitchen?

Gel is applied in small spots inside cracks and hiding places, away from food and work surfaces. We tell you where it has been placed.

My neighbours have cockroaches. Will mine come back?

In apartment blocks cockroaches move through shared drains and ducts. We treat entry points and recommend the building arranges treatment of shared areas.

How many visits will it take?

German cockroaches usually need a follow-up visit two to four weeks after the first. Heavy infestations may need more.
