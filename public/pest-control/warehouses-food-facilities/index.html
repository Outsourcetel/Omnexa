What we cover

Pest control for storage and production

Warehouses and food facilities hold stock for long periods, with large doors, pallets and loading bays. That suits rodents, stored-product insects and birds. A programme here protects your stock and the paperwork your customers and auditors ask for.

  • Perimeter and internal rodent monitoring
  • Stored-product insect inspection and control
  • Fumigation of stock, containers and structures where needed
  • Bird control at loading bays and roofs
  • Loading-bay and door proofing advice
  • Trend reports and audit file

Stored-product pests

Why stock gets infested, and what to do

Beetles, moths and weevils live in grain, flour, dates, spices, pet food and packaged goods. Warm conditions let them breed quickly, and a small infestation in one pallet can spread through a store.

The answer is a combination: check incoming goods, rotate stock so nothing sits for long, clean spills and residue from floors and racking, and keep the store tidy enough for inspection. Where an infestation is established, targeted treatment or fumigation may be needed.

Storage practice

Habits that reduce infestation

These practices make the biggest difference to warehouse pest risk:

  • Keep pallets away from walls to leave an inspection gap
  • Rotate stock so the oldest goods leave first
  • Sweep and remove spilled product and packaging debris
  • Do not store waste or damaged goods inside
  • Keep dock doors closed when not loading
  • Inspect incoming goods and reject or isolate infested consignments

Audit file

Records customers and auditors expect

Keep these together and up to date:

  • The contract and scope of service
  • A site plan showing every monitoring point
  • Service reports for each visit
  • Trend analysis of activity
  • Records of any fumigation, including clearance
  • Product list with labels
  • A log of corrective actions and who closed them

When fumigation is needed

If stored goods or the structure are infested, fumigation may be the right tool. It is planned with a site assessment, sealing, an exposure period and aeration, and nobody enters until the air has been tested and we have issued written clearance.

FAQ

Frequently asked questions

How do stored-product insects get into stock?

They are often already in the goods when they arrive, or enter from infested residues in the building. Warm conditions let them breed quickly, so inspection of incoming stock and clean storage matter.

Do you offer fumigation for warehouses?

Yes. Fumigation is planned with a site assessment, sealing, an exposure period, aeration and written clearance before anyone re-enters.

Can you work around our shifts and deliveries?

Yes. We plan visits around receiving and dispatch and agree access to each area with your supervisors.

What pest records do customers and auditors ask for?

Usually the contract, a site plan with monitoring points, service reports, trend analysis and a log of corrective actions. We keep this file complete.

We store food and non-food goods. Does that change the plan?

Yes. Food areas get stricter product control and monitoring-based methods. We map the zones with you and set the rules for each.
