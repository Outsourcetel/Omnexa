Bird Control: what we do

Birds nesting on buildings leave droppings that stain and corrode surfaces, block gutters, ducts and air-conditioning units, and create hygiene risks. The lasting fix is to stop them landing and nesting, not just to move them on.

  • Inspection of roosting and nesting sites
  • Netting of balconies, shafts, atriums and loading bays
  • Spikes, tensioned wire and other landing deterrents
  • Removal of nests and cleaning of droppings
  • Proofing of plant rooms, ducts and AC platforms
  • Advice on removing food and water that attract birds

Why it matters

The problems birds cause

Bird problems are rarely only about appearance.

  • Droppings that stain facades and corrode metal and stonework
  • Slip hazards on walkways and entrances
  • Nests that block gutters, drains, ducts and AC units
  • Droppings and feathers contaminating food, packaging and water tanks
  • Noise at dawn and dusk
  • Complaints from guests, tenants and customers

Cleaning droppings is a health task

Dried bird droppings carry dust that can be breathed in. We clean heavy deposits in protective equipment and bag the waste, so your staff or tenants are not exposed.

FAQ

Frequently asked questions

Which birds cause the most problems?

Pigeons are the most common on buildings. Sparrows and starlings cause problems in warehouses, food sites and atriums.

Are bird droppings dangerous?

Dried droppings can carry disease organisms and release dust when disturbed. Cleaning should be done with protective equipment, which is why we do it as part of the job.

Do you harm the birds?

Our approach is proofing and deterrents. The aim is to stop birds landing and nesting, not to poison them.

How long does netting last?

Good netting that is correctly fixed lasts for years. The mesh size and the fixing points matter more than the price.

Will the birds just move to the next building?

They can. Proofing the nesting and roosting sites you control, and removing food, is what stops them returning to yours.
