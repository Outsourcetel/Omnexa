Local conditions

Pest pressure in Riyadh

Riyadh's dry climate and very hot summers push pests towards the places people keep cool and watered. Rodents and cockroaches move into air-conditioned buildings, and irrigated gardens keep the soil moist enough for termites.

The city is also building quickly. New villas and commercial buildings are the best time to apply anti-termite soil treatment, because it is far cheaper before the slab is poured than after damage appears.

What we treat most

Common call-outs in Riyadh

Termites in villas

Door frames, wardrobes and kitchen cabinets attacked from the soil beneath the slab.

Rodents in compounds and warehouses

Activity around waste areas, loading bays and landscaped grounds.

Cockroaches in kitchens

German cockroaches in restaurant and home kitchens, American cockroaches from drains.

Bed bugs

In homes after travel, and in hotels and staff accommodation.
