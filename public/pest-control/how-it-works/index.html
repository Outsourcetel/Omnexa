How our pest control works

From the first call to the follow-up visit: what happens, who does what, and what you get at each step.

WhatsApp usCall +966 55 818 7808
Steps1. Request an inspection2. Site inspection3. Written plan and quotation4. Treatment5. Service report6. Follow-up and monitoring
1
We reply during business hours

Request an inspection

What we do

Take your enquiry, note the site, the pest and a convenient time, and confirm the visit.

What you do

Tell us what you have seen, the type of site, and who will give access.

What you get

A confirmed visit time and the technician's name.

2
Typically 30 to 90 minutes

Site inspection

What we do

Inspect inside and outside, identify the pest, find entry points and the conditions that attract it, and take photos.

What you do

Give access and point out where you have seen activity. Tell us about children, pets and food areas.

What you get

Findings explained on the spot, in plain language.

3
Same visit or shortly after

Written plan and quotation

What we do

Write up the method, the products, the preparation needed, the number of visits and the price.

What you do

Review the scope, ask questions, and approve.

What you get

A written quotation. Nothing starts before you agree.

4
Depends on site size and pest

Treatment

What we do

Carry out the treatment in protective equipment, place and number bait stations, and seal gaps where agreed.

What you do

Complete the preparation steps and keep people and pets out of treated areas for the stated time.

What you get

Re-entry time and safety instructions, before we leave.

5
Handed over after every visit

Service report

What we do

Leave a written report: findings, areas treated, products used with their registration numbers, and recommendations.

What you do

Keep the report with your records. Food and hospitality sites will need it for audits.

What you get

A dated, signed service report.

6
Timed to the pest's life cycle

Follow-up and monitoring

What we do

Return for the follow-up visit, check activity, and adjust the plan. Contracts get scheduled visits and trend reviews.

What you do

Tell us if you see any activity between visits.

What you get

An updated report and, for contracts, a trend summary.
