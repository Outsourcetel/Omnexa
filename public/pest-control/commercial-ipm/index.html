Commercial IPM Programme: what we do

Commercial sites need ongoing control, not one-off treatments. An IPM programme combines inspection, prevention, monitoring and targeted treatment, with documentation.

  • Risk assessment and site survey
  • Monitoring devices and trend reporting
  • Scheduled service visits
  • Documentation for audits (e.g. food-safety schemes)
  • Staff awareness guidance
  • Coverage for food, hospitality, healthcare, retail and logistics sites

The IPM cycle

Assess, prevent, control, then assess again

Assessment

Survey the site, identify pests and risks, and set a baseline.

Prevention

Proof the building, improve sanitation and storage, and remove what attracts pests.

Control

Treat only where monitoring shows activity, using the lightest effective method.

Review

Trend the monitoring data, check the plan works, and adjust it.

FAQ

Frequently asked questions

What is IPM?

Integrated Pest Management combines prevention, monitoring and targeted treatment to control pests while limiting unnecessary chemical use.

Can you support our food-safety audits?

We provide service reports and records. Which audit requirements apply depends on your scheme, so tell us what you are audited against.

Do you also help with ISO 22000 or HACCP?

Yes, our ISO consultancy team can help with food-safety management systems. See the ISO consultancy section.

How often will you visit?

It depends on the risk. Food production and kitchens are commonly visited more often than offices or retail. We propose a frequency after the survey.

Can you take over from our current contractor?

Yes. We review the existing file and monitoring points, then set up our own records so there is no gap.

Do you attend audits?

We can be present or on call during your audit to answer questions about the pest control programme.

Do you cover several branches?

Yes. Multi-site contracts use the same reporting format at each site so you can compare them.
