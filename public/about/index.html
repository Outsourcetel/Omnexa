About Omnexa Solutions

Pest management, ISO consultancy and training, and facility services for businesses in Saudi Arabia.

Who we are

What we do

Pest management. Integrated Pest Management, with pesticide and non-pesticide methods, for homes, food sites, hotels, hospitals and warehouses.

ISO consultancy and training. Gap analysis, documentation, internal audit and training for ISO standards and food-safety schemes.

Facility services. Air-conditioning, construction, safety signage and equipment, and IT.

Our offices are in Riyadh (Al Olaya) and Jeddah (Al Handawiya).

Our mission

To give clients dependable service, and to make sure our staff, products and equipment meet the standards our clients set.

Our vision

To be the pest management and facility services provider that clients choose for reliability and value.
