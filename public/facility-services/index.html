How we work

The same discipline on every service

Inspect first

We look at the site or the system before we quote, so the scope matches what is really there.

Written scope

What is included, what is not, and the timing are written down before work starts.

Records

You receive a written record after each visit or milestone, which you can keep for your own files.

One point of contact

One team to call for a leaking AC, a sign that needs replacing or a pest sighting.

Quotation

What we need to prepare your quote

Request a quote and tell us as much as you can. A photo or a short voice note on WhatsApp is often enough to start.

  • The location and type of building
  • Which service you need, and any deadline
  • The number and type of units or areas involved
  • Photos of the equipment, area or problem
  • Whether the work must be done out of hours
  • Any site rules, permits or access arrangements we should know about

Scope is agreed in writing

Each job or contract has a written scope. If we find something during a visit that is outside it, we record it and tell you, so you can decide how to handle it.

Process

How it works

  1. Site inspectionWe visit the building or the systems involved, check their condition and note what you need.
  2. Written scope and quotationYou receive a written scope of work and a quotation. Nothing starts before you agree.
  3. Work or programme startsWork is carried out to the agreed scope and schedule, with your site rules respected.
  4. Records and follow-upYou receive a written record of what was done, and follow-up visits where the scope includes them.
