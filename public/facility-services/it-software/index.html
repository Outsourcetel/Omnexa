Preparing

What to prepare before we speak

You do not need a technical specification. A short description is enough:

  • The problem you want to solve, in plain words
  • Who will use the system and roughly how many people
  • The tools and systems it must connect to
  • Examples of the reports or screens you expect
  • Language requirements, such as Arabic and English
  • Your target date, and any rules about where data may be stored

Connecting systems

What system integration means in practice

Most businesses run several systems: accounting, inventory, sales, customer records. When they do not talk to each other, staff re-type data and mistakes creep in.

Integration connects them, so that an order entered once appears where it is needed. It begins with a map of what data is held where, followed by agreeing which system is the source of truth for each item, and then building and testing the connections.

AI

Where AI features can help

AI is a tool, not a goal. It suits tasks that involve language, documents and pattern recognition. Examples:

  • Searching and summarising large sets of documents
  • Sorting incoming requests or emails
  • Drafting standard replies for a person to review
  • Extracting details from forms and invoices
  • Answering routine questions from a knowledge base

Agree ownership early

Before work starts, agree in writing who owns the source code, where the data is hosted, who can access it and how support will work afterwards. We include these points in the quotation.

FAQ

Frequently asked questions

What kinds of software do you build?

Custom software, web and enterprise applications, AI-powered applications, and integrations between existing systems.

Can you connect our existing systems?

Yes. System integration links systems such as accounting, inventory, customer and reporting tools so that data does not need to be entered twice.

What do you need from us to start?

A description of the problem, who will use the software, what it must connect to and your target date. A rough list is enough for a first conversation.

Will we own the software?

Ownership, hosting and support arrangements should be agreed in writing before work starts. We will set them out in the quotation.

Do you build AI features?

Yes, AI-powered applications are part of what we offer. We start from the business task, and tell you honestly whether AI is the right tool for it.
