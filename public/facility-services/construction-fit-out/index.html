Coordination

Why MEP coordination matters

Mechanical, electrical and plumbing services share the same ceilings, walls and risers. If each is planned separately, they clash on site, and the fix is slow and disruptive.

Coordination means the services are planned together with the structure and the interior layout, so routes, levels and access panels are agreed before work starts. It also decides the order of the trades, so that finished work is not opened up again. For fit-out, it includes checking that AC layouts, lighting and fittings match the ceiling design.

Before we start

How to prepare a fit-out brief

A clear brief saves time and reduces changes later. Gather what you can of the following:

  • The address, the unit size and any existing drawings
  • How the space will be used and by how many people
  • Your required finishes and any brand or design requirements
  • Your target start and opening dates
  • Landlord or building management rules for contractors and working hours
  • Any equipment that needs special services, such as extra AC load

Closing out

What you receive at handover

Handover is when the project becomes yours. You should receive:

  • A completed snagging list showing each item closed
  • Records of the works carried out
  • Information on installed equipment and the AC and services layout
  • Guidance on maintenance of installed finishes and equipment

FAQ

Frequently asked questions

What types of project do you take on?

Civil and structural works, MEP coordination, interior fit-out, and industrial and commercial buildings. Send us the scope and location and we will confirm whether it suits us.

What does MEP coordination mean?

MEP stands for mechanical, electrical and plumbing. Coordination means planning those services together with the building works so they fit, in the right order, without clashes.

Can you fit out a shop or office from an empty unit?

Yes. Interior fit-out covers the internal works to turn an empty or existing space into one ready for use, to your design and brief.

Do I need drawings before you quote?

Drawings help, but are not essential for a first visit. We can start from a site visit and your brief, and tell you what drawings will be needed.

Who handles approvals and permits?

It depends on the project and the authority involved. Tell us the scope and location at the start and we will explain what applies and who is responsible for each item.
