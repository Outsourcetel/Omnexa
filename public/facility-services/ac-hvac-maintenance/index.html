Saudi conditions

Why summer is the test for an AC

Long running hours

Units run for many hours a day through the hot months, so wear builds up faster and a small fault can turn into a breakdown at the worst time.

Dust and sand

Filters and coils clog with dust, particularly in inland areas such as Riyadh. Less airflow means weaker cooling and more strain on the compressor.

Humidity on the coast

In humid coastal areas such as Jeddah, units remove a lot of moisture. Blocked drains and wet coils are common causes of leaks and smells.

Service before the season

A service in spring finds weak parts while a repair can still be scheduled calmly, instead of during the hottest weeks.

Early warning

Signs your AC needs attention

Call for a check if you notice any of these:

  • Weak or uneven cooling
  • Ice on the indoor unit or the refrigerant pipes
  • Water leaking from the indoor unit
  • Unusual noises such as rattling, buzzing or squealing
  • A musty smell when the unit starts
  • The unit switches off by itself or trips the supply
  • The outdoor unit is very noisy or does not run

Refrigerant

Gas refilling: why a unit loses gas

An AC or refrigerator uses a sealed circuit. The refrigerant is not consumed, so if the level is low, gas has escaped through a leak, usually at a joint, a valve or a damaged pipe.

Refilling without finding the leak gives only a short-term fix, because the gas escapes again. We check for the leak, tell you where it is and what it takes to repair, and then recharge the unit. The same applies to refrigerators and deep freezers, which we also refill.

Installation

AC installation and ducting: what to get right

Unit size and position

A unit should suit the room size, the sun exposure and the use of the space. Positions should allow airflow and service access.

Duct routing and sealing

Short, smooth duct runs with sealed joints lose less air. We plan routes together with other building services.

Insulation

Ductwork and refrigerant pipes should be insulated, particularly where they pass through hot spaces.

Drainage and power

Condensate lines need a proper fall and a safe discharge point, and the unit needs a supply suited to its rating.

Maintenance contracts

What an annual or preventive maintenance contract includes

A contract replaces emergency repairs with planned visits. A good one is written down:

  • A register of every unit, with location and type
  • A visit schedule agreed with you, set by the number of units and how hard they run
  • A checklist completed at every visit
  • A written report after each visit, with any faults and recommendations
  • Call-out arrangements between planned visits
  • Clear wording on what is covered and what needs a separate quotation, such as major parts

FAQ

Frequently asked questions

How often should an AC be serviced?

At least once a year, ideally before the summer starts. Units that run most of the day, or that sit in dusty or humid locations, benefit from more frequent filter cleaning. We recommend a schedule after seeing the units.

My AC is running but not cooling. What could be wrong?

Common causes are dirty filters or coils, low refrigerant because of a leak, a failing fan or compressor, or an outdoor unit that cannot release heat. A technician needs to check the unit to tell which.

Can you refill the gas?

Yes, for AC units, refrigerators and deep freezers. Gas does not get used up, so a unit that is low on gas has a leak. We look for the leak and explain the options before refilling.

Do you offer maintenance contracts?

Yes. Annual and preventive maintenance contracts cover scheduled visits to all your units, a checklist and a written report. Request a quote with the number and type of units.

Do you install new units and ducting?

Yes. Tell us the rooms, the building type and whether it is a new installation or a replacement, and we will arrange a site visit.
